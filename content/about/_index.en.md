I’m an electrical engineer and have spent a lot of time delving into a wide variety of control engineering topics. I’ve been able to apply this expertise to projects involving electric vehicles from various manufacturers—from working on high-voltage components to helping design software for electric drivetrains.

In the process, I discovered something else: my passion for great software. I love automating processes, simplifying workflows, and developing features that truly make a difference.


> Trained as an electrical engineer, shaped as a software developer, driven by a passion for DevOps.

- **Coding Stack**: WezTerm, Zsh, Zellij, Neovim, Yazi, Lazygit, Lazydocker, K9S
