Bikepacking in Sweden and Denmark 2025

A record of my bikepacking trip through Denmark and Sweden

Blog#

Here I’m documenting my bike trip from Flensburg, through Copenhagen, across Sweden, and back through Denmark to Flensburg.

Start: July 1, 2025

You can find the preparations for this trip, as well as reports on individual days, in separate posts here:

Conclusion

Impressions I’ve now been traveling through Denmark, Sweden, and Germany for 21 days. Of those, I spent about 2 days on the train, took a day off in Sweden, and spent a day with friends in Cologne. Unfortunately, southern Sweden didn’t really win me over on this solo trip. The Kattegattleden West Coast Bike Trail ran mostly through tourist areas, industrial zones, or along major roads. While this wasn’t entirely unexpected, it unfortunately turned out to be true. It’s easy to escape the hustle and bustle inland. Just a few kilometers from the coast, you’re cycling alone through the forest, far away from people, supermarkets, and campsites. However, as a solo traveler, I didn’t get to experience that for more than a few days. That’s why I decided to take the last ferry before Oslo, back to Denmark. ...
